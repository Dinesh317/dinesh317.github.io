About

Who I Am

I'm a Data Scientist specializing in building and scaling end-to-end machine learning systems. My core strength is personalized recommendation — I led the architecture and deployment of a real-time engine at Jio Platforms now serving over 8 million users across JioCinema and JioSphere, delivering low-latency content using fine-tuned BERT embeddings, Kafka streaming, and vector search.

Before Jio, I spent three years at AgEYE Technologies applying computer vision to precision agriculture — deploying Mask R-CNN and EfficientNet models on NVIDIA Jetson edge devices, achieving 94% segmentation accuracy and cutting manual inspection time by 60%.

I think about the full ML lifecycle as a single, continuous problem: from data engineering and model training through to production deployment and monitoring. Whether it's a PySpark ETL pipeline, a FAISS-powered ranking engine, or TensorFlow Lite inference on mobile — I care deeply about systems that work reliably at scale.
