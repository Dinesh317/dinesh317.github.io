About Me

I'm a Data Scientist specializing in building and scaling end-to-end machine learning systems. My core focus is personalized recommendation engines — I led the design and deployment of a real-time system at Jio Platforms that now serves over 8 million users across JioCinema and JioSphere.

Before that, I spent three years at AgEYE Technologies applying computer vision to precision agriculture, deploying deep learning models on edge hardware (NVIDIA Jetson) that reduced manual plant inspection time by 60% and achieved 94% segmentation accuracy on real-world datasets.

I'm equally comfortable designing an ETL pipeline with PySpark and Kafka, fine-tuning a BERT model for semantic search, or optimizing inference for resource-constrained devices. I think about the full ML lifecycle — data, model, and deployment — as one continuous problem to solve.
